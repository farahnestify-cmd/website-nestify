About Nestify

A smart home brand founded in 2024, built on a simple belief — technology should feel invisible, and living should feel effortless.

We design each project around the client and the space, bringing all compatible systems together in one simple and reliable setup. Our focus is clean design, smooth integration and dependable performance — from installation to ongoing support.

  • 01

    Clean design

    Every interface, switch and sensor chosen to complement your interiors — never compete with them.

  • 02

    Smooth integration

    Lighting, curtains, climate and security working together as one system, not a collection of apps.

  • 03

    Dependable performance

    Reliable installations backed by an expert team and ongoing technical support.
